# 🍔 Food Delivery Analytics Project

**Comprehensive data analysis of a food delivery service**  
*(Real transactional data – 15k+ orders, 25k+ events)*

---

## 📖 Overview

This project performs a deep‑dive analysis of a food delivery platform to assess business health, identify growth opportunities, and uncover operational risks.  
The analysis answers key business questions:

- How are audience and revenue growing?
- How well does the service retain customers?
- Which customer segments are most valuable?
- How efficient is the logistics (delivery, cancellations)?
- Are there imbalances in courier hiring?
- What are the key unit‑economics metrics (ARPU, ARPPU, AOV)?

The work is based on **real anonymised data** from a two‑week period and applies **cohort analysis**, **RFM segmentation**, and **unit‑economics calculations**.
